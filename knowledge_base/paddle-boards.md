# Paddle boards

## Sizes
We sell three inflatable paddle boards. Pick the size by rider weight, including any gear.
- 10'6" All-round: riders up to 85 kg. Best for beginners and calm water.
- 11' All-round: riders up to 110 kg. The most stable board, also good for two light riders.
- 12'6" Touring: riders up to 130 kg. Faster and longer, for trips and experienced paddlers.
A rider between two sizes should take the larger board.

## What is in the box
Every board comes with: the board, a 3-piece adjustable paddle, a double-action hand pump with gauge, a removable center fin, a coiled leash, a valve wrench, and a carry bag.
Part numbers: fin PB-FIN, pump PB-PUMP, paddle PB-PADDLE, leash PB-LEASH, valve wrench PB-WRENCH, carry bag PB-BAG.

## Inflation
Inflate to 12 to 15 PSI. Below 12 PSI the board bends in the middle.
Losing 1 to 2 PSI overnight is normal when the air cools down. That is not a leak.

## Valve check for slow leaks
Most slow leaks come from a loose valve core, not from the board. Ask the customer to do this first:
1. Inflate the board to 15 PSI.
2. Spray soapy water on the valve. Bubbles mean air escapes there.
3. Put the valve wrench on the valve core and turn it a quarter turn clockwise.
4. Spray again, then check the pressure after 24 hours.
If the board still loses more than 2 PSI in 24 hours, ask for the order number, the board size, and how many days ago the board arrived.

## Problems and what happens next
A colleague handles every shipment. Never promise one in a reply.
- Missing accessory: we ship the missing part. No return needed.
- Slow leak: valve check first.
- Still leaking after the valve check, within 30 days of delivery: we send a new board.
- Still leaking after the valve check, more than 30 days after delivery: we send a patch kit.
- Split seam: always goes to a colleague.
To arrange anything, we need the order number, the board size, and a short description of the problem.
