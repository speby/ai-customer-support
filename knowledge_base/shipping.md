# Shipping

## Orders
Amazon ships every order. Delivery dates and tracking are in Your Orders on Amazon. We cannot see or change an Amazon delivery.

## Replacement parts
Replacement parts and kits ship from our own warehouse, free of charge. They leave within 2 business days and arrive in 3 to 7 business days. The customer receives a tracking link by email when the parcel leaves.

## Countries
We sell in the countries where our products are listed on Amazon. Replacement parts ship to the same address as the original order.
