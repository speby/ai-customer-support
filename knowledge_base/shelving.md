# Shelving

## Models and finishes
We sell four flat-pack models, each in four finishes: natural oak, white, black, and walnut.
- HP-S3: 3-shelf bookcase, 106 cm high.
- HP-S5: 5-shelf bookcase, 176 cm high.
- HP-C4: cube unit 2x2, 77 cm high.
- HP-C9: cube unit 3x3, 112 cm high.
The model code is printed on the assembly manual and on the box label.

## Parts lists
Every panel has a number sticker. The part number is the model code plus the panel number, for example HP-S3-04.
- HP-S3: 1 left side, 2 right side, 3 top, 4 shelf, 5 bottom, 6 back panel.
- HP-S5: 1 left side, 2 right side, 3 top, 4 to 6 shelves, 7 bottom, 8 back panel, 9 kick plate.
- HP-C4: 1 top, 2 bottom, 3 left side, 4 right side, 5 center divider, 6 shelf, 7 back panel.
- HP-C9: 1 top, 2 bottom, 3 left side, 4 right side, 5 and 6 dividers, 7 and 8 shelves, 9 back panel.
Hardware kits: HK-S for the S models, HK-C for the C models. A kit holds all cam locks, dowels, screws, and the wall anchor.

## Assembly tips
- Lay the panels out on the box or a rug, so they do not scratch.
- Cam locks: the arrow on the cam lock points at the hole for the cam bolt. Turn clockwise until it stops, about half a turn.
- The back panel goes in with the finished side facing into the shelf.
- Tall units (HP-S5, HP-C9) must be fixed to the wall with the included anchor.
- Assembly takes 30 to 60 minutes with one person. A cordless screwdriver on a low setting is fine.

## Problems and what happens next
A colleague handles every shipment. Never promise one in a reply.
- Missing screws or hardware: we ship the hardware kit for the model.
- 1 or 2 damaged panels: we ship those panels by part number.
- 3 or more damaged panels, or a damaged frame: we send a full replacement.
- Wrong finish delivered: we exchange it for the right finish.
To arrange anything, we need the order number, the model, and the part numbers of damaged panels. A photo helps.
